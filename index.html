Hermes Gmail

Hermes Gmail is a personal assistant integration that connects to your Gmail account, with your explicit consent, so the Hermes assistant can help you read, search, and organize your own email.

What it does

  • Accesses your Gmail only after you sign in with Google and grant permission.
  • Uses that access solely to perform actions you request.
  • You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Read our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
